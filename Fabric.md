SOL MVMT - Brand Identity & Voice Guide

BRAND OVERVIEW
Brand Name: Sol Mvmt (Sol Movement Collective) Website: solmvmt.com Location: San Tan Valley, Arizona Contact: centered@solmvmt.com | 123-456-7890
Brand Essence: Sol Mvmt is a wellness collective that bridges the physical and spiritual through intentional movement practices. We create space for transformation through yoga, Lagree, and functional fitness—but our deeper mission is nurturing the soul alongside sculpting the body.

BRAND VOICE & TONE
Voice Characteristics:
Contemplative yet accessible - We explore depth without pretension
Warm and grounding - Like a trusted guide, not a distant guru
Introspective and poetic - We find beauty in the ordinary
Honest and human - We acknowledge struggle alongside growth
Intimate and personal - We speak as a friend, not a corporation
Tone Spectrum:
Inspirational (60%) - Uplifting reflections on life, presence, mindfulness
Educational (25%) - Teaching yoga philosophy, movement principles
Invitational (15%) - Welcoming people to classes, events, community
What We Sound Like:
"Between the breaths" not "Maximize your workout"
"Come as you are" not "Transform in 30 days"
"The sacred hiding in the ordinary" not "Wellness hacks"
"Your body already knows" not "Push harder"
Lowercase brand name: "sol mvmt" (casual, approachable)
What We Don't Sound Like:
Corporate wellness jargon
Hustle culture / no pain no gain
Perfectionist fitness messaging
Overly mystical or inaccessible spiritual language
Sales-heavy promotional content

CAPTION FORMAT & STRUCTURE
Content Types:
1. Reflective Posts (Most Common) Format:
Opening: Poetic observation or question
Body: 2-3 short paragraphs exploring the theme
Close: Invitation to reflect or gentle CTA
Length: 100-180 words
Hashtags: 8-12, focused and relevant
Example:
Between the inhale and exhale, there is a moment of perfect stillness.

In that pause, you're neither holding on nor letting go. You simply are.

This is where clarity lives. This is where you meet yourself.

Today, find one moment between the breaths.

#SolMvmt #BetweenTheBreaths #YogaPhilosophy #MindfulLiving
2. Story-Based Posts Format:
Begin with a personal or family story
Connect to universal truth or lesson
Tie back to practice or philosophy
Length: 150-220 words
3. Invitation Posts (Classes/Events) Format:
Brief poetic opening (2-3 lines)
Clear event details (day, time, location)
What to bring / what to expect
Welcoming close: "Come as you are"
Length: 80-120 words
4. Educational Posts (Yoga/Movement) Format:
Introduce concept or pose
Explain benefits or philosophy
Practical application
Keep accessible, not technical
Length: 100-150 words
Hashtag Strategy:
Primary (Always include 3-5): #SolMvmt #YogaPhilosophy #MindfulMovement #BetweenTheBreaths #PhoenixYoga
Secondary (Rotate 5-8): #YogaArizona #SanTanValleyYoga #YogaCommunity #FunctionalFitness #LagreeMethod #OutdoorYoga #YogaFlow #IntentionalLiving
Engagement (Use 2-3): #YogaEveryday #MindBodySoul #YogaLife #WellnessJourney
Topic-Specific: Add 1-2 based on post theme (e.g., #JoyBeyondGrief, #GenerationalWisdom)

Perfectionism in imagery or language
Overly polished, influencer-style content
Trend-chasing for engagement
Controversial hot takes
Political or divisive content
Anything that doesn't feel authentic to Kelly's voice
